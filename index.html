GOOD QUESTIONS

Wondering
where to start?

Here are a few things you might want to know. Still unsure? We can talk it through.

Ask me anything
I'm a complete beginner. Is this for me?
Absolutely. You don't need to be fit to start. We'll begin at your level, focus on the foundations and build your confidence as you go.
What happens in the free consultation?
It's a relaxed conversation about your goals, experience, lifestyle and any obstacles you've faced. You'll have the chance to ask questions and see whether coaching is a good fit. There's no obligation to sign up.
Do I need to train every day?
No. The best routine is one you can follow consistently. We'll look at your schedule and find a sensible starting point, whether that's a couple of sessions a week or something different.
Will I have to follow a strict diet?
Not at all. My approach is to help you understand nutrition and build practical habits, rather than rely on restrictive diets or quick fixes. Nutrition education stays within my professional scope.
Where does the coaching take place?
I'm based in Greater Manchester. We can discuss suitable training locations and available support options during your consultation.
How much does personal training cost?
That depends on the level of support you need. We'll discuss available options and pricing openly during the free consultation, with no pressure to commit.
